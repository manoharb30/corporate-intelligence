3. **Snapshot vs SignalPerformance drift.** `SnapshotService.detect_clusters` rebuilds clusters from raw transactions every call (`window_end = latest tx date`), while `process_incremental` anchors clusters at first formation and updates in place. After we wrote fresh blobs, running uvicorns (local + Railway) continue serving stale until TTL expires or process restarts. User saw stale data even after blob refresh — we did not verify whether prod or local.
